  Cuesta, H., Gómez, E., Martorell, A., Loáiciga, F. (2018). Choral Singing Dataset [Dataset].

  Paper: "Analysis of Intonation in Unison Choir Singing", Proceedings of the 15th International
  Conference on Music Perception and Cognition (ICMPC), 2018.

Sung by singers of the Anton Bruckner Choir, Barcelona. One singer per part.

Changes: held notes cut from the individual close-microphone recordings of "Locus Iste",
"Niño Dios" and "El Rossinyol", consonants trimmed, levelled, a loop crossfade baked in, folded
to mono and encoded to AAC by pipeline/quartet.py in the Chorale Workshop repository. Pitch is
centred in the app at playback; the recordings themselves are not retuned.
