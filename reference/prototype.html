Every pause lands on a plain chord

All eight fermatas fall on a simple major or minor triad, so the clash gauge drops to zero at each one. The friction builds in the middle of the phrase, where passing notes in the inner voices rub against each other.

Minor at the start, major at the end

It opens on D minor and finishes on F major. Phrase six, on “Ehr und Zier” (honour and adornment), closes on a bright D major chord rather than the minor one you might expect.

The tune was a love song

Hans Leo Hassler published the melody in 1601 as a secular song. Bach set it several times across the Passion, and Paul Simon reused it for “American Tune” in 1973.
